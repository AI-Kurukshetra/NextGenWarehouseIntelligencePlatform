# Core Features Specification

## 1 Inventory Tracking & Management
Real-time visibility of inventory across all locations.

Capabilities:
- lot tracking
- serial number tracking
- expiration date tracking
- multi-location support

---

## 2 Receiving & Put-Away

Workflow:
Receive shipment → inspect → scan → assign storage location.

---

## 3 Order Management

Features:
- order creation
- wave planning
- pick list generation
- order prioritization

---

## 4 Pick Path Optimization

Algorithm calculates optimal picking route.

Goal:
Minimize worker travel time.

---

## 5 Cycle Counting

Capabilities:
- scheduled counts
- variance tracking
- automatic adjustments

---

## 6 Shipping & Packaging

- label generation
